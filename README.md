# Supply-Chain-Inventory-Analysis
# 📦 **Inventory & Supplier Performance Dashboard**

## 📌 Overview

The **Inventory & Supplier Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze inventory levels, supplier performance, product demand, delivery efficiency, revenue, and profitability. It provides a centralized view of key supply chain metrics, enabling businesses to monitor stock availability, evaluate supplier reliability, and optimize inventory management through data-driven insights.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

This dashboard provides valuable insights into inventory and supplier performance, including:

* **Revenue & Profit Overview**

  * **Total Revenue:** **2.5 Billion**
  * **Total Profit:** **4.1 Million**

* **Inventory Status Analysis**

  * Compares **Units Sold** against **Stock Quantity** for each product.
  * Products such as **Mixer, Laptop, and Camera** exhibit high sales volumes, highlighting strong customer demand.
  * Inventory levels remain relatively balanced across products, supporting effective stock management.

* **Supplier Performance**

  * Supplier contributions are evenly distributed, with **Supplier A** and **Supplier C** contributing slightly higher inventory volumes.
  * No single supplier dominates the supply chain, reducing dependency risk.

* **Delivery Performance**

  * Average delivery delays range from **5.39 to 5.55 days**.
  * **Supplier C** records the shortest delivery delay, while **Supplier E** has the highest average delay, indicating opportunities to improve logistics efficiency.

* **Top Selling Products**

  * **Camera** is the highest-selling product with approximately **240K units sold**.
  * **Laptop** and **Mixer** closely follow, while **Shoes** record the lowest sales among the listed products.
